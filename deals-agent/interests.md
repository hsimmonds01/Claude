# Deals & Codes — what counts as genuinely good

Edit this file directly to retune what clears the bar. Plain English — the
ranker reads it as-is on every run.

## The bar

A push should feel like a friend spotted something and messaged you, not
like an ad. Most checks should find nothing worth sending — that's correct,
not a failure.

**Good:**
- A price that's genuinely rare for that exact product, not just "cheaper
  than usual by a few pounds."
- A discount code that's plausible and specific (a named retailer, a
  believable amount off), especially for Deliveroo, Uber Eats, or general
  retail.
- Free-to-enter promos or perks that add real value with low effort — the
  kind of thing worth knowing about even though nothing's "for sale."

**Be skeptical of / skip:**
- "Was £X now £Y" claims where £X looks like an inflated RRP rather than a
  price anyone actually paid recently.
- Generic low-value tat that's only "trending" because it's cheap, not
  because it's good.
- Codes with no real specifics — anything that reads like a template
  ("SAVE10", "10% off site-wide" with no retailer named) rather than
  something a real person actually found and posted.

## Leans toward interesting (not a hard filter)

Tech and gadgets, especially affordable/well-reviewed ones. Food and
takeaway discounts, including Deliveroo/Uber Eats codes. General retail
codes are fine too if the discount is real and specific.

## Leans toward skip (not a hard filter)

Baby/child products, DIY/trade tools, medical/health devices, gambling or
betting promos — none of these are relevant, so don't strain to find a
reason to include one.
