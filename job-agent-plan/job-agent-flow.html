🧠  Score every role

An AI reads each job against three plain-English files she controls, and marks it out of 10:

cv.md — her CV, as text
profile.md — what she wants: titles, salary floor, location, dealbreakers
feedback.md — standing rules she keeps, plus quick reactions that fade over time

A job is scored once, the first time it's seen, and that mark sticks — so nothing drifts up and down day to day.
